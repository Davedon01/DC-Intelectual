<h1 className="text-4xl md:text-5xl font-serif leading-tight">
The Four Temperaments: Understanding Human Personality
</h1>

<p className="text-lg leading-relaxed text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
Human beings have always tried to understand why people behave differently.
One of the oldest personality frameworks is the <strong>Four Temperaments theory</strong>,
which classifies personalities into four main types:
<strong> Sanguine, Choleric, Melancholic,</strong> and <strong>Phlegmatic</strong>.
</p>

<p className="text-lg leading-relaxed text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
Although modern psychology has developed more complex models,
the four temperaments remain popular because they are simple,
intuitive, and easy to understand.
</p>

</div>

---

## What Are the Four Temperaments?

<p className="leading-relaxed text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
The Four Temperaments are personality types originally linked to the balance
of bodily fluids (called <em>humors</em>) in ancient medicine. These temperaments
describe general patterns of behavior, emotional responses, and ways people
interact with the world.
</p>

<div className="bg-neutral-100 dark:bg-neutral-900 p-6 rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800 mt-6">

<h3 className="font-semibold mb-3">The Four Types</h3>

<ul className="list-disc ml-6 space-y-1">
<li>Sanguine</li>
<li>Choleric</li>
<li>Melancholic</li>
<li>Phlegmatic</li>
</ul>

</div>

<p className="mt-6 leading-relaxed text-neutral-700 dark:text-neutral-300">
Most people are not purely one temperament. Instead, they usually have
a <strong>dominant temperament</strong> with traits from one or two others.
</p>

---

# 1. Sanguine Temperament

<p className="leading-relaxed">
The <strong>Sanguine</strong> personality is typically energetic,
social, and optimistic.
</p>

<div className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-6">

<div className="bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-green-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Common Traits</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Outgoing and talkative</li>
<li>Enthusiastic and lively</li>
<li>Friendly and approachable</li>
<li>Enjoys being around people</li>
<li>Often spontaneous</li>
</ul>

</div>

<div className="bg-green-50 dark:bg-green-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-green-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Strengths</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Great communicators</li>
<li>Easily make friends</li>
<li>Positive outlook on life</li>
<li>Motivating and inspiring</li>
</ul>

</div>

</div>

<div className="bg-red-50 dark:bg-red-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-red-200 mt-6">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Weaknesses</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Can be impulsive</li>
<li>May struggle with discipline</li>
<li>Sometimes forgetful or disorganized</li>
</ul>

</div>

<p className="mt-6 leading-relaxed">
Sanguine individuals often thrive in environments involving
people, creativity, and excitement such as
<strong> sales, entertainment, and public speaking.</strong>
</p>

---

# 2. Choleric Temperament

<p className="leading-relaxed">
The <strong>Choleric</strong> personality is associated with
leadership, ambition, and determination.
</p>

<div className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-6">

<div className="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-blue-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Common Traits</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Goal-oriented</li>
<li>Strong-willed</li>
<li>Confident</li>
<li>Independent</li>
<li>Decisive</li>
</ul>

</div>

<div className="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-blue-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-blue-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Strengths</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Natural leaders</li>
<li>Highly motivated</li>
<li>Good problem solvers</li>
<li>Focused on results</li>
</ul>

</div>

</div>

<div className="bg-red-50 dark:bg-red-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-red-200 mt-6">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Weaknesses</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Can be impatient</li>
<li>May appear controlling</li>
<li>Sometimes insensitive to others</li>
</ul>

</div>

<p className="mt-6 leading-relaxed">
Choleric personalities excel in
<strong> leadership roles, management, entrepreneurship,
and competitive environments.</strong>
</p>

---

# 3. Melancholic Temperament

<p className="leading-relaxed">
The <strong>Melancholic</strong> personality is thoughtful,
analytical, and detail-oriented.
</p>

<div className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-6">

<div className="bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-purple-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Common Traits</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Deep thinkers</li>
<li>Organized and structured</li>
<li>Sensitive and emotional</li>
<li>Perfectionistic</li>
<li>Loyal and dependable</li>
</ul>

</div>

<div className="bg-purple-50 dark:bg-purple-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-purple-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Strengths</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Highly analytical</li>
<li>Strong attention to detail</li>
<li>Creative and reflective</li>
<li>Very dedicated to work</li>
</ul>

</div>

</div>

<div className="bg-red-50 dark:bg-red-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-red-200 mt-6">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Weaknesses</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Can overthink situations</li>
<li>May struggle with pessimism</li>
<li>Sometimes overly critical</li>
</ul>

</div>

<p className="mt-6 leading-relaxed">
Melancholic individuals excel in fields requiring
<strong>analysis, creativity, and precision</strong> such as
writing, research, design, and planning.
</p>

---

# 4. Phlegmatic Temperament

<p className="leading-relaxed">
The <strong>Phlegmatic</strong> personality is calm, patient,
and reliable.
</p>

<div className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-6">

<div className="bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-yellow-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-yellow-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Common Traits</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Peaceful and relaxed</li>
<li>Patient and tolerant</li>
<li>Good listeners</li>
<li>Consistent and dependable</li>
<li>Avoids conflict</li>
</ul>

</div>

<div className="bg-yellow-50 dark:bg-yellow-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-yellow-200">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Strengths</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>Excellent mediators</li>
<li>Loyal friends</li>
<li>Emotionally stable</li>
<li>Supportive and caring</li>
</ul>

</div>

</div>

<div className="bg-red-50 dark:bg-red-950 p-6 rounded-lg border border-red-200 mt-6">

<h4 className="font-semibold mb-3">Weaknesses</h4>

<ul className="list-disc ml-5 space-y-1">
<li>May avoid decisions</li>
<li>Can resist change</li>
<li>Sometimes lack motivation</li>
</ul>

</div>

---

## Can Someone Have More Than One Temperament?

<p className="leading-relaxed">
Yes. Most people are a <strong>blend of multiple temperaments</strong>.
For example:
</p>

<ul className="list-disc ml-6 space-y-1">
<li><strong>Sanguine–Choleric</strong>: outgoing leader</li>
<li><strong>Melancholic–Phlegmatic</strong>: thoughtful and calm</li>
<li><strong>Choleric–Melancholic</strong>: driven but analytical</li>
</ul>

---

## Why Understanding Temperaments Matters

<div className="bg-neutral-100 dark:bg-neutral-900 p-6 rounded-xl border border-neutral-200 dark:border-neutral-800">

<ul className="space-y-2">
<li><strong>Self-awareness</strong> – understanding strengths and weaknesses</li>
<li><strong>Communication</strong> – recognizing different interaction styles</li>
<li><strong>Teamwork</strong> – appreciating diverse personalities</li>
<li><strong>Personal growth</strong> – improving emotional intelligence</li>
</ul>

</div>

<p className="mt-6 leading-relaxed">
By understanding temperament differences,
people can develop stronger relationships
and collaborate more effectively.
</p>
